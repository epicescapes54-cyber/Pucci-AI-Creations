The Profile

Creative direction,
amplified by AI.

Pucci AI Creations was born from a simple belief: the tools changed, but taste still matters.

I've spent years building brands the traditional way — the moodboards, the photoshoots, the late-night edits. AI didn't replace any of that. It removed the bottleneck. Now a concept that used to take three weeks and a crew takes an afternoon, and the creative energy goes where it belongs: into the idea.

My work sits at the intersection of art direction and applied AI — generative imagery, video, copy systems and automations, all built on top of a real brand strategy so the output looks like you, not like a prompt.

Content Engine

A reusable prompt library and content calendar that lets a small team publish daily without losing the brand voice. These are real prompts from the kit — tap any one to read it, then drop your email to copy it.

Product Shot Editorial product photograph of {PRODUCT} on a soft blush satin surface, warm gold rim light, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, subtle shadow, luxury e-commerce styling, clean negative space at top for text, photorealistic, 4k
Lifestyle Scene Candid lifestyle photo of a confident Black woman wearing {PRODUCT}, golden hour light through a window, natural skin texture, warm neutral wardrobe, 35mm, film grain, authentic expression, editorial magazine quality
Reel Hook Script Write 5 opening hooks (under 8 words each) for a 20-second vertical video about {TOPIC}. Voice: warm, confident, a little playful. No hype words, no emojis. Each hook must create an open loop the viewer needs closed.
Carousel Captions Turn {TOPIC} into a 6-slide carousel. Slide 1 = the hook. Slides 2–5 = one idea each, max 14 words. Slide 6 = a soft CTA to {ACTION}. Then write a 2-line caption and 5 niche hashtags.
Brand Voice Guard Rewrite the following in the {BRAND} voice: warm, grown, encouraging, never salesy. Short sentences. No exclamation points. Speak to one person, not an audience. Keep every factual claim intact: {TEXT}
Weekly Calendar Build a 7-day content calendar for {BRAND}. Each day: format (reel / carousel / story / static), the hook, the core message, and the CTA. Rotate through: education, behind-the-scenes, product, social proof, personal story.
